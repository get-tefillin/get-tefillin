Tefillin Now is a free app that connects anyone who wants to put on tefillin with a nearby volunteer who brings a kosher pair. Tap, watch the volunteer approach on the map, and lay tefillin — at home, at work, on the street or at the airport. Looking for tefillin near me? Tefillin Now finds the closest volunteer. There is nothing to pay; you can donate if you like. Have tefillin? Join as a volunteer and help Jews near you do the mitzvah.
